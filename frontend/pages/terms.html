This experience is a design prototype. It cannot send money, scan a real QR code, perform NFC payments, verify a merchant or provide a live exchange quote.

Merchant names, transactions, conversion rates, fees and references shown here are illustrative sandbox data. They are not offers, receipts or financial advice.
