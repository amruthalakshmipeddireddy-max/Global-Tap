Privacy

What this prototype does

This static prototype demonstrates payment and travel screens. It does not connect to a bank, payment partner, camera, location service or merchant network.

Information entered here

Questions and converter amounts remain in the open page for the current session. They are not saved by this prototype or sent to a server.

Before a real launch

A production service would need a full privacy notice covering identity checks, transaction records, location permissions, payment partners, retention, security and user rights under applicable law.

Prototype notice: this project is a design prototype with sandbox data only. No real payments are processed.
